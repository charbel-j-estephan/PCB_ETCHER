16x2 LCD Display Stand by Thejustintimer on Thingiverse: https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:1904529

Summary:
After trying to mount my lcd screen rigidly and upright I decided to add a stand to the original design by deisterhold. This design features a 30 degree incline and a method of securing the screen in place which requires no supports. This method uses a bar to slide into position as I'm sure you can work out from the included files.
